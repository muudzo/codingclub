## About the game

Line up at the starting gate, hit dirt jumps, and race through a three-round championship across sunset, forest, and floodlit night tracks.

## How to play

1. Tap **Play now** above—nothing to install.
2. **On a computer:** use **W / Up arrow** to go, **S / Down arrow** to brake, and **A / D** (or **Left / Right arrows**) to lean in the air. Press **R** to get back on the track if you crash, and **P** or **Esc** to pause.
3. **On a phone or tablet:** use the on-screen **GAS**, **BRK**, and **◀ / ▶** buttons.
4. **Tip:** try to land flat on both wheels with the slope for a speed boost, and keep your helmet off the dirt!
